# Release And Outcomes

## S7 Release Readiness

Verify reproducible artifact/version; environment/config differences and secret requirements without values; dependencies/licenses; migration/cutover/validation/rollback; monitoring/alerts/owners; demonstrated backup restore; runbook/support/staged rollout; and authority for external mutations.

G7 passes only when release and recovery are executable and failure will be noticed.

## S8 Delivery

Without production authority, deliver a deployable candidate with exact reproduction and evidence. With authority, execute the frozen plan, record steps, observe health, and rollback at agreed thresholds. Do not broaden rollout based on early optimism.

`DELIVERY_REPORT.md` distinguishes verified completion, completed but not deployed, blocked, deferred/out of scope, unverified, accepted risk, version/evidence, and recovery.

G8 passes when the artifact is identifiable, reproducible, recoverable, and every claim matches evidence.

## S9 Outcome Review

After sufficient real observation, compare with baseline and separate adoption, usability, business, reliability, and operational findings. Account for confounders. Conclude expand, correct, observe longer, rollback, or stop. Usage volume, generated features, and enthusiasm alone do not prove the problem was solved.
